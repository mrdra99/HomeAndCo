# Department icons

Original Home & Co. vector artwork. Thirteen department-specific symbols on a shared 48 × 48 grid, with a 2-unit stroke, rounded terminals, navy (#0b2a47) outlines and magenta (#d11559) functional details.

Each department has a distinct symbol: cookware, armchair, cleaning spray, cosmetics, pet bowl, plant, stationery, gift, wrench, light bulb, bathtub, car and die. SVGs are served locally without an icon package or external requests.
